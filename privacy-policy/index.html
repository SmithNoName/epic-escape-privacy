2. Why information is processed

Information is processed to operate and maintain the Game, save your preferences and progress, select an appropriate advertising provider for your region, display and measure advertising, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose technical problems, improve performance, and comply with legal obligations.

Depending on your country, availability, and consent choices, the Game may use the following third-party services. These providers process information under their own privacy policies:

An advertising provider may use cookies or similar technologies, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and device information to deliver, limit, secure, and measure ads. Personalized advertising is used only where permitted. In regions where consent is required, the Game requests consent before requesting personalized advertising. You may still receive non-personalized or limited ads if you decline.

4. Your privacy choices

Where available, you can review or change Google advertising privacy choices from the Game's Settings menu. You can also reset or delete your device advertising identifier and control ad personalization in your Android settings. Clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling it removes locally stored game preferences and progress.

You may withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

5. Legal bases and international processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, information is processed with your consent, to provide the Game you requested, to comply with law, or for legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and improving the Game, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. They are responsible for using appropriate safeguards where required by applicable law.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is overwritten, cleared, or the Game is uninstalled. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. To request access to or deletion of information controlled by us, contact us using the address below. We may need information sufficient to verify and process the request. For data controlled independently by a service provider, you may also need to contact that provider directly.

7. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.
